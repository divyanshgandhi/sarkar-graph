## Scope
Explorer surface for Sarkar Graph (`/` → Union wheel, `/[gov]` state wheels, entity routes). Mode: Operate (the visitor completes a task: find a seat, understand it). Audience, job and constraints: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The Indian state as one surveyed wheel. The People sit at the centre as a 24-spoke chakra; every seat is placed by its distance from the voter; selecting a seat turns the wheel until it rests at the foot and draws its chain of authority home. Refuses the org-chart tree and the card dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: CivLab's warm paper-and-ink world (khadi paper by day, warm near-black by night), flat inks, Anek Latin + Anek Devanagari. Flag colours only as hairlines and marks: saffron is the People and every "elects" line; green is Parliament; chakra navy is the executive; ochre the courts. Sector colour maps territory, never state.
STORY: See the whole state at once → find a seat → learn who put it there, whom it answers to, what changed.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (news, changes, survey stamp); full-height wheel right; search top-left; Graph | Power | States switch bottom-right.
FORM: #7 of 7, Survey-of-India sheet grammar (tier rings, layer toggles, sheet-index breadcrumb, legend key, surveyed/unsurveyed). Seed de26f956.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Raises (from declined challengers)
- Tensegrity column: every seat shows a distinct state (filled, acting, additional charge, vacant, unverified); no state is colour-only.
- Iridescent cloud edge: colour stays confined to hairlines, edges and small marks; text fields stay achromatic.
- Phosphor terminal: fully keyboard-driven (/ search, arrows walk siblings, Esc up a level); the change log prints like a transcript.
- One-bit desktop: going from the Union into a State animates lineage (the state's node expands into its own wheel).
- Copy-shop zine: sector colour marks territory only; selection and focus use a separate ink.
- WPA poster: flat inks, no gradients; every view carries one plain-language caption line.

## Memorable moment
Click any seat: the wheel turns (spring, ~700ms) until the seat rests at 6 o'clock, and a saffron line runs from the chakra through every appointing hand to it.
