# Union ministries & departments — canonical IDs and research groups

Starting list (from the Allocation of Business Rules as understood in mid-2024). **Verify against the current Allocation of Business Rules / cabsec.gov.in / india.gov.in and correct it**: if a ministry/department was created, merged, renamed or moved since, follow reality and say so in `notes`. Keep the ID pattern (`in-min-<slug>`, `in-dept-<slug>`).

Where a ministry has only one department, the ministry entity is the unit (no separate `in-dept-*` needed) and its Secretary heads it administratively.

## Group A — Core / economic
- `in-min-finance` — depts: `in-dept-economic-affairs`, `in-dept-expenditure`, `in-dept-revenue` (CBDT, CBIC), `in-dept-financial-services`, `in-dept-investment-and-public-asset-management` (DIPAM), `in-dept-public-enterprises`
- `in-min-corporate-affairs`
- `in-min-commerce-and-industry` — `in-dept-commerce`, `in-dept-promotion-of-industry-and-internal-trade` (DPIIT)
- `in-min-statistics-and-programme-implementation`
- `in-min-planning` (NITI Aayog sits under it — NITI itself is owned by the core segment)
- `in-min-micro-small-and-medium-enterprises`
