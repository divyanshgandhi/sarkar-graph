## Export baseline and data-model gap

- `gaps.csv`: **788** `missing_district_head` and **252** `missing_city_heads` records. District gap detail says districts have *no seats yet*; a gap ID stands for more than one needed office (Collector/DM and SP), so filling the gap text alone cannot create the graph relationships. `people.csv` has **zero** rows whose `body_id` contains `-dist-`.
- `people.csv`: **24** mayor seats, **23** named holders, **zero** photos. The named Jammu mayor row is a particular currency risk below. Existing Delhi MCD mayor and commissioner rows are confirmed by [MCD's current homepage](https://mcdonline.nic.in/), but their CSV citations need replacing with that primary source.
- District Collector/DM and municipal commissioner/mayor are distinct offices on distinct legal bodies. For example, Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban are separate collectorates, while BMC is a municipal corporation. GHMC's legal territory also crosses district boundaries; do not make its commissioner a district head.

## Critical structural omission: Hyderabad split in February 2026

Telangana [G.O.Ms.No.55 of 11 February 2026](https://tg-bn-website-assets.flowwlabs.tech/GOs-and-ACTs/GO.Ms.No.55_11-02-2026.pdf), linked from the [state BuildNow order index](https://buildnow.telangana.gov.in/go-and-act/), says in clause 2 that existing GHMC is reorganised into **three separate municipal corporations**. Clause 3(iv) makes each a separate body corporate; clause 4 makes the order effective **immediately**, hence **11 February 2026**. The legal names and areas are:

| Legal body from the order | Zone coverage | Export state | Current commissioner from [Telangana MA&UD directory](https://www.telangana.gov.in/departments/municipal-administration-urban-development/) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation | Shamshabad, Rajendernagar, Charminar, Golconda, Khairatabad, Secunderabad | `st-tg-mc-greater-hyderabad` (`G02956`); body is present with Wikipedia-only source and no commissioner seat | Sri R.V. Karnan, IAS |
| Cyberabad Municipal Corporation | Serilingampally, Kukatpally, Qutbullapur | `st-tg-mc-cyberabad` (`G02957`); body is present with Wikipedia-only source and no commissioner seat | Smt. G. Srijana, IAS |
| Malkajgiri Municipal Corporation | Malkajgiri, Uppal, L.B. Nagar | `st-tg-mc-malkajgiri` (`G02958`); body is present with Wikipedia-only source and no commissioner seat | Sri T. Vinay Krishna Reddy, IAS |

The current state directory lists all three commissioners on one page, but its footer is a literal `[last-modified]` placeholder, so there is **no usable page date**. The legal entities and effective date are grounded in the dated order; the current holders are one-official-source candidates pending another check. [GHMC's own About page](https://www.ghmc.gov.in/) still says 650 sq km, six zones and 150 wards, and its [contacts](https://www.ghmc.gov.in/KeyContacts.aspx) were crawled from a months-old version. These pages conflict with the order and should not define the 2026 legal boundary. The [HYDRAA state portal](https://hydraa.telangana.gov.in/about) independently lists the same three present corporations and zone groups. The order copies the collectors of Hyderabad, Ranga Reddy, Sangareddy and Medchal Malkajgiri, demonstrating why one municipal body must not be attached exclusively to Hyderabad District.

**Graph change needed:** all three body IDs already exist in `data/raw/states-ka-ap-tg.json`; replace their shared Wikipedia-only citation and generic legal description with G.O.Ms.No.55 and its zone boundaries. Add commissioner seats with the directory citations. GHMC's mayor section is empty on its old contact page; an empty page does **not** establish a legal vacancy or name an administrator. Check a current election/administration order before recording mayor status.

## Remaining uncertainties and next source checks

1. Get formal **posting orders** for Bengaluru Urban to resolve P.S. Kantharaju versus G. Jagadeesh. Keep `st-ka-dist-bangalore-urban` unresolved until then.
2. Verify current **municipal election/administrator orders** for GHMC, Cyberabad, Malkajgiri, Jammu and Srinagar before asserting elected mayor vacancies or filling mayor seats. A blank municipal webpage or historical mayor profile is insufficient.
3. Find start dates and SP holders separately. None of the district rows above establishes a district SP; `missing_district_head` combines that need with DM/Collector and thus understates the number of missing seats.
4. Treat official page footer dates cautiously. The Telangana MA&UD directory lacks a working timestamp, and JMC retains a mayor page at odds with later governmental reporting. The project should store **fact observed date, source publication/update date, and tenure date** separately.
