For example, the source `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjay_Seth_(Jharkhand_politician)` became a truncated URL. ## How to verify at scale

- **Current membership:** Compare Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha seat rows against [Digital Sansad's current Lok Sabha member list](https://sansad.in/ls/members) and [Rajya Sabha member list](https://sansad.in/rs/members), including membership status. Their member pages can also help identify portraits. Use [ECI's results and election records](https://www.eci.gov.in/general-elections) for the election outcome and party at election time; results alone do not prove that a member still occupies a seat in September 2026.
- **Current executive office:** Use current Cabinet Secretariat portfolios, presidential appointment/resignation communiqués, Gazette notices, and each body's current official roster. Keep date of action separate from date the page was accessed.
- **States and local bodies:** Use current state government directories, official orders, and district/municipal sites. A stale directory must not override a later transfer, election, or reorganisation order.
- **Photos:** Match the image's actual subject to the official holder and seat. Record the original file page and reuse terms; a thumbnail URL or Wikipedia article link alone does not establish either.

The `as_of` value in each seat is the graph compilation snapshot, not a per-fact verification date. The generated coverage document now describes roster breadth without claiming that all holders received an independent official-source check. The raw research still carries many `high` labels; the compiled graph applies the Wikipedia-only downgrade until an eligible source is attached.
