## Dataset-wide data-quality implications

- **6,058 of 6,786 `high` people rows cite only Wikipedia/Wikimedia URLs** (962 Union; 5,096 State/UT). This includes 31 chief ministers and 27 governors. Calculated by parsing every nonempty `sources` value in `people.csv` and requiring all URL hosts to end in `wikipedia.org` or `wikimedia.org`. The repository policy says Wikipedia alone cannot support `high`; these are citation/confidence corrections even where the holder is factually right.
- **5,982 of 7,774 seats have no `photo`** (1,068 Union; 4,914 State/UT). All **1,792** nonempty photo URLs use Wikimedia hosts. `gaps.csv` has no image gap type, so image coverage is currently invisible to its help queue. Official rosters above offer identifiable portrait sources, but image identity, freshness and reuse rights need a separate check.
- The current sources are event-volatile. The [PMO officers list](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/list-of-officers-pmo/) was updated **2026-09-01**, while the [PM Council asset roster](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/assets-and-liabilities-of-the-union-council-of-ministers-2025-2026-as-on-31-03-2026/) is explicitly **as of 2026-03-31**. A March list must not be treated as a September full Cabinet verification.

## Unresolved in this pass

The audit did not verify all 7,774 seats or all 3,403 gaps. In particular, the full Union ministerial roster, all 36 State/UT councils, MLA/MP membership changes, and 2026 governor assumption dates need systematic official roster reconciliation. The specific `since` dates for Mundayur/AICIL, Vakil/SFIO and West Bengal CM should await appointment or oath records; the `since` values for several confirmed gaps should remain blank until then.
