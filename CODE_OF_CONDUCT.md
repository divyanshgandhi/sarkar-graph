# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Adapted, briefly, from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
with an added political-neutrality clause specific to this project.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, caste, or political affiliation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences — especially on a project that maps
  political institutions and touches on Indian party politics.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, particularly about data accuracy.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the accuracy and usefulness of the dataset and the community as a whole.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information — physical or email address, phone number, or any other identifying
  detail — without their explicit permission. This applies to fellow contributors; it does not apply to public
  officials' official-capacity information in the dataset itself, which is governed separately by
  `docs/SOURCING_POLICY.md`.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Political neutrality clause

This project maps the structure and office-holders of the Government of India. It does not take positions on
parties, politicians, or policy. In this community:

- Contributions, reviews, and discussion should stay focused on **factual accuracy and sourcing**, not on
  political opinion or advocacy for or against any party, politician, or government.
- No contributor may use their access (code, data, or maintainer status) to introduce bias, privileged
  treatment, or de-prioritization of any party or individual — see `docs/SOURCING_POLICY.md` §5 for the
  concrete rules this maps to in the data itself (colour, ranking, and framing).
- Disagreements about a fact are resolved by evidence and sourcing quality, never by political alignment.
- Off-topic political debate (as opposed to sourcing disputes about specific facts) does not belong in this
  project's issues, PRs, or other community spaces.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will
take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
official communication channel for this project), and also applies when an individual is officially representing
the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.
*(Contact channel to be added by the maintainer before publishing — e.g. a dedicated email address or a private
reporting form. Until then, report via a private message to a maintainer on GitHub.)* All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow the Contributor Covenant's standard graduated response — correction, warning, temporary
ban, permanent ban — proportionate to the severity and pattern of a violation. See the full
[Contributor Covenant v2.1 enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
for the reference text this project adopts in full for non-political-neutrality matters.
